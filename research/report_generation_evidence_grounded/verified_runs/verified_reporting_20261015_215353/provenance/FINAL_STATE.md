## Notebook 12A — matched-coverage reliability

**COMPLETE**

At matched external coverage, uncertainty provided a useful but modest
selective reliability signal.

Mean segmentation-risk reduction relative to the full target set was positive
for all three methods.

Uncertainty–error correlations were directionally positive across all seeds
and methods and were stronger for lesion-extent error than for segmentation
error.

These findings support uncertainty as a selective evidence-eligibility signal,
but do not establish universal calibration or universal superiority of one
uncertainty estimator.
